A note to remember

One place for the details you keep looking up.

Was that a bottle or a breastfeed? What texture did we try yesterday? Keeping food and milk entries together makes it easier to look back without searching different notes.

Use the log to describe your day. It does not need to become a timetable, a target or a record of every minute.

What to record for each type of feed

For every entry
Date, time and a short note if there is something you want to remember.
For a bottle
Milk type and the amount you recorded. Use the same unit each time and say whether the amount was offered or taken.
For breastfeeding
Feed time and duration, if you want to record it. Leave a volume blank when it is not known.
For food
Food or ingredients, texture, approximate amount and your baby's response.

In TinyTaste, use notes to clarify details such as “offered” versus “taken.” The app cannot measure a feed for you.

A daily log you can copy

Start with a few headings in a notebook or phone note. Add one line per event. These blank rows are a template, not a suggested number or order of feeds.

TimeMilk or foodDetailsNotes
________________Amount, duration or texture________
________________Amount, duration or texture________
________________Amount, duration or texture________

Date: __________ · Record estimates as estimates. Add rows as needed.

Keep numbers in context.

A duration is not a measured milk volume. A food amount entered from memory is an estimate. A blank entry does not necessarily mean a feed was missed.

Keep those distinctions in your notes so the record remains useful when you revisit it. Avoid adding a guessed volume just to complete a total.

A feeding tracker cannot establish whether your baby is getting enough milk or food, assess growth or set a feeding plan. Discuss those questions with a qualified healthcare professional who knows your baby.

What TinyTaste puts together

TinyTaste lets you log food with texture, amount, response and notes, and record milk type, amount, duration and time in the same app.

TinyTaste is for iPhone. Food and milk history is stored on your device, without live sharing between caregivers or automatic log restoration through account sign-in. Basic baby profile details can be restored when signed in. Some personalised features process profile and log data remotely; see our privacy policy.
