Thinking & attention

What helps someone focus, understand and learn?

HUMAN QUESTION
Attention, memory, perception and cognitive effort during a specific task.
OBSERVATIONS
Task responses, eye movements, response times and self-reported effort; EEG or fMRI in a suitable research setting.
VARIABLES
Response latency, error patterns, gaze allocation and task-specific effort ratings.
ANALYSIS
Compare task conditions, check reliability and test how indicators relate to performance.
INTELLIGENCE
Adapt pacing, explanation or task difficulty to evidence about the learner.

Gaze duration or a brain signal does not directly reveal attention or understanding. Interpretation requires context and validation.

WAYS OF OBSERVING

Self-reports & interviews · Digital behavior · Wearables & trackers · Speech & interaction · Physiological signals · EEG, fMRI & eye-tracking
